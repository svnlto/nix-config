# Lean ADR template (6 sections)

A lightweight format for small or personal decisions that do not warrant the
full SF template. For Software Factory / accor platform work, prefer the SF
template (`sf-template.md`).

```markdown
---
type: decision
date: YYYY-MM-DD
domain: work
project: accor
status: proposed
tags: [adr, ...]
---

# ADR - 00XX - Title

## Revision

| Date       | Version | Note          | Author |
| ---------- | ------- | ------------- | ------ |
| YYYY-MM-DD | 0.1     | Initial draft | Name   |

## Initial Situation

What exists today, why it matters now, and the problems with the current
state. Acknowledge that the current state has worked; the people who built it
are the audience.

## Decision

What was decided and the key constraints. Frame as a proposal, not a
conclusion.

## Consequences

What changes as a result, positive and negative. Tables over prose.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Strength | Why not, for now |
|-------------|----------|------------------|
| ... | ... | ... |
```
